Haunted Tenancy

Game Design Document — v1.2
Genre: 2D Horror Comedy Investigation
Platform: Godot Engine / Pixel Art

Elevator Pitch: You're a cheapskate tenant in a cursed multi-floor building. Rent is impossibly low because every room is haunted. You don't know which entity haunts which room. Investigate across floors to piece together symptoms, research on your laptop, buy the right exorcism items, and survive when entities breach their confinement. Save $2,000 to escape. Don't die.

1. MDA Framework

Mechanics (Rules)

  • Core verb: Room investigation — enter room, click objects, find anomalies, manage danger.
  • Resources: Money ($), Time (day timer), HP (20 base), Inventory (exile items).
  • Progression: Rooms unlock per week. Entities cross-floors — same entity may haunt multiple rooms.
  • Constraints: Danger meter (0-100), Suspicion meter (0-100), curse debuffs, no skip, all rooms must be addressed.

Dynamics (Behavior)

  • Risk/reward: Spend on items to survive combat, or save faster but risk spiral.
  • Information game: Unknown entities, cross-room symptoms, piecing together which entity haunts which rooms.
  • Time triage: More rooms = less time per room. Must rotate through floors or danger accumulates.
  • Carelessness cascade: Spam clicks → suspicion + paranoia → worse investigation → more combat.

Aesthetics (Experience)

  • Tension: Ever-rising danger meter. Entity could breach at any moment.
  • Discovery: Clues across rooms reveal entity identity. Detective satisfaction.
  • Dark humor: Bureaucratic horror framing. Landlord treats entities as "amenities."
  • Competence: Learning patterns, optimizing investigation routes across floors.

Navigation

  • Floor tabs at top of the map: [BASEMENT] [FLOOR 1] [FLOOR 2] [ATTIC]
  • Stair cells in the grid: click to move up/down one floor
  • Switching floors is instant, no time cost

Floor-Specific Danger

Danger rises faster in darker or more confined floors. Basement (+20%) is riskier but yields more valuable clues. Attic (+10%) is cramped and harder to navigate. Floor 1 and 2 are standard. This encourages strategic routing — clear dangerous floors quickly before the timer runs out.

5. Danger Meter & Suspicion System

Two independent meters govern the investigation:

Room Danger (0-100)

DANGER METER — Demon's Study
Idle rise: +1.0/sec baseline (basement +20%, attic +10%)
Miss anomaly: +15 per unseen anomaly (auto-added on leaving room)
Wrong click: +6 per normal object clicked
Correct click: -15 per anomaly found
Curse penalty: +30% faster with Paranoia or Fatigue
0-39: SAFE 40-69: WARNING 70-99: CRITICAL 100: COMBAT

Suspicion (0-100)

SUSPICION METER — Tracks How "Noticeable" Player Is
Wrong click: +8 per normal object
Spam click: +10 per click within 400ms of previous
Consecutive misses: +15 on 3rd wrong click in a row
Correct anomaly: -5
Leaving room: reset to 0
0-24: Safe 25-49: Rising 50-79: High 80-99: CRITICAL 100: ATTACK

Both meters operate simultaneously. Danger is the primary threat (time-based, unavoidable if you stay). Suspicion is optional — skilled players investigate cleanly with low suspicion, while careless players face additional combat threats.

7. Entity Identification & Cross-Room Clues

Core tension: You don't know which entity haunts each room. Symptoms for the same entity may appear in multiple rooms across different floors. Detective work is required.

Unknown Entities at Start

  • Floor plan shows room names only — no entity icons initially.
  • Entering a room: ENTITY: ??? — investigate to identify.
  • Anomalous objects reveal symptom clues (e.g. "sulfur smell", "chalk marks").
  • Clues are global — they persist across rooms and days in your journal.

8. Random Combat Event

Combat is not player-initiated. It triggers as a random event when either the danger meter OR the suspicion meter hits 100 during investigation. The entity breaches confinement and attacks.

Combat Trigger Conditions

TriggerThresholdResult
Danger reaches 100Too long / missed anomalies / high floorEntity attacks — combat overlay appears
Suspicion reaches 100Too many wrong clicks / spamEntity notices you — attacks immediately

Combat Flow

Combat is turn-based, rock-paper-scissors style. Time is paused during combat. Enemy telegraphs its move → player picks correct counter-item from inventory. Wrong item = damage + item wasted. Correct = ritual advances. 4 correct counters + EXORCISM = victory.

13. Narrative & Tone

Genre: Dark comedy, bureaucratic horror.
Core joke: You are not heroic. You're a miser hunting entities in a multi-floor building because rent is $200/month. For that price you'll live with actual demons on multiple floors.

Tone inspirations:

  • Papers, Please — bureaucratic dread
  • What We Do In The Shadows — supernatural mundanity
  • House of Leaves — unsettling domestic multi-floor horror

Sample Flavor Text

  • Landlord email: "Basement has 2 presences. We're calling it a 'theme.' No rent change."
  • Entity description: "Standard Revenant Type B. Non-violent unless provoked. Provoked = sharing symptoms across floors."
  • End-of-day: "The Study and Kitchen both smell of sulfur. Probably related. Definitely not your problem at $200/month."

14. Edge Cases & Balance

RiskMitigation
Curse death spiral Sunday rest, mercy mechanic (3rd wrong click = warning first), emergency loan
All-in-one strategy Items are entity-specific. No universal item. Cross-room entities force rotation.
Overwhelming late game Banished rooms stay safe permanently. Player clears dangerous floors first.
Boring early game Tutorial day has surprise combat (Ghost breaks confinement). Immediate stakes.
Combat trivialization Prep quality gates tells. Must earn clues + buy items. Memory check under pressure.
Wiki = walkthrough Clues are partial — never 100% match. Red herrings from other entities. Only 2/5 symptoms needed.
Floor hopping exploit Unvisited floors accumulate danger overnight. Must rotate or face enraged entities.
Runaway economy $2,000 goal with $5-$30 items creates strategic spending tension. 25-40 day target.

17. Unique Selling Points

  1. Unknown entities across floors: You don't know what haunts each room. Symptoms for one entity may appear in multiple rooms on different floors — detective work required.
  2. Two-phase tension: Investigation phase (find clues, manage danger) → Random combat phase (use knowledge to fight). Both phases affect each other.
  3. Carelessness punished: Spam clicks, misclicks, and rushing cause suspicion buildup → paranoia curse → false clues. Skilled play is clean play.
  4. Knowledge-based combat: World of Horror style ritual exile. No grinding — you win by correctly identifying the entity and memorizing its banish sequence.
  5. Compounding consequences: Curses alter how you perceive and interact with the game. Paranoia shows fake anomalies. Vision curse hides real ones.
  6. Dark comedic tone: Bureaucratic horror. Landlord treats entities as "amenities" and "energy density."
  7. Strategic floor routing: Basement rises danger 20% faster. Attic 10% faster. Must decide which floors to clear first. Unvisited floors = enraged rooms tomorrow.
